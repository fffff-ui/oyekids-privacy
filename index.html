Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information submitted through our Facebook lead forms, ads and wholesale inquiry channels.

Effective date: October 27, 2026 Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who we are

Oyekids Clothes Wholesale (“we”, “us” or “our”) is a wholesale supplier of children’s clothing based in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China. We use Meta lead forms and related advertising services to receive and respond to wholesale inquiries.

For the purposes of this policy, we are the controller of the personal information you submit through our lead forms, unless a separate agreement says otherwise.

2. Information we collect

Contact information Name, phone number, and email address if you choose to provide it.
Business inquiry details Your location, buyer type, expected order quantity, preferred styles, and any information you send us about your wholesale needs.
Communications Messages, quote requests, feedback, and records of our follow-up conversations on Facebook, WhatsApp or other channels.
Advertising and form data Information provided by Meta about your interaction with our ads or lead form, such as form submission events and campaign-level engagement data, subject to Meta’s own policies.

We do not ask for payment card details, identity documents, passport information or visa information through our lead forms.

3. How we use your information

  • Respond to your wholesale inquiry and send requested stock, size and pricing information.
  • Prepare shipping, pickup or export quotations and arrange follow-up.
  • Communicate with you about your order, samples, availability or market visits.
  • Maintain business records and improve our products, advertisements and customer service.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse of our forms, or other unlawful activity.
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger about your inquiry. You can ask us to stop marketing communications at any time. We will still respond to messages that are necessary to complete a transaction you requested or to meet a legal obligation.

4. Legal basis and consent

Where required by law, we process your information based on your consent, our legitimate business interests in responding to wholesale inquiries, the performance of a contract or steps taken before entering into a contract, and compliance with legal obligations.

By submitting our lead form, you consent to us contacting you about your inquiry. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, subject to applicable law.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share it only as necessary with:

  • Meta Platforms: to operate and receive submissions from our Facebook lead forms, subject to Meta’s privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp and communication providers: to follow up on your inquiry.
  • Hosting and IT service providers: who support our business systems and protect their security.
  • Logistics, inspection or export partners: where you ask us to arrange a shipment or service.
  • Professional advisers and authorities: where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

These providers are expected to protect personal information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

6. International processing

Our business is based in China. Your information may be processed in China and in other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure that cross-border processing is subject to appropriate safeguards.

7. Retention

We keep lead and inquiry records only for as long as necessary to respond to your request, manage our business relationship and meet legal or accounting obligations. Unless a longer period is required by law, we normally delete or anonymize lead records after 24 months from the last meaningful contact.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information.
  • Stop or limit certain communications.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

10. Cookies and third-party links

This policy page is a static page and does not use advertising cookies or analytics scripts. Links to Facebook, WhatsApp or other third-party services are governed by those providers’ own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them before submitting information.

11. Children

Our wholesale services and lead forms are intended for adult business buyers. They are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through these channels.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements or data practices. When we do, we will post the updated policy on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
